>*“Your word is a lamp to guide my feet and a light for my path.”* Psalm 119:104-105

Dear Family and Friends, 

We have two recent journeys to share with you that we experienced God’s presence, guidance and blessings. Firstly for Yong, he joined a prayer ride into the south/southeastern borderlands of Cambodia, with lots of river crossings and border crossings. As usual this was a ride to prayer through the land, to pray for God to plant and build up churches, to pray for redemption and freedom in places of despair and oppression. One border town particularly felt dark and uncomfortable, where the team were looking for accommodation for the night and decided to circle back out of the town to sleep undisturbed. Cambodia is a nation that fears and appeases spirits, horror ghost films are the most popular entertainment, people live with spirit houses and daily offerings. Would you pray with us that Jesus, the light of the world, would enter into the darkness here, to bring release, hope, freedom from fear?

Secondly, Wendy gathered at the start of May at a hotel in Phnom Penh to complete facilitation of a cohort 18 christian workers, both internationals and Khmer, who longed to deepen their faith and spiritual maturity, through formative guided retreats, bible study and prayer. Facilitating this cohort has been a weekend and evening commitment for Wendy and four other leaders over the past eight months or so, with a combination of large and small group meetings, 1:1 coaching, and through both online and in person gatherings. At the final retreat we celebrated together as each person recognised their refreshed identity in Christ, the ways they understand God gives them values, gifts and a vision in which to serve Jesus, which of course for each person is unique. If this sounds interesting to you, do ask us to share more! This year was the first time we had some Khmer believers joining. We hope and pray that in the future God will grow a desire and capacity for Khmer believers to translate and contextualise these materials to be a wider blessing to the Khmer church. The Khmer church is very young, generally first generation, lacking mature elders, there is a desire and need for discipleship to grow elders and mature believers. Would you pray with us for God to grow maturity and commitment to discipleship within the Khmer church?
